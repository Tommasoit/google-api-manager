Private, owner-operated utility

Tao Lin Business Reports

Read and review authorized Google business account data from the owner's computer.

This Python desktop utility helps Tao Lin (林涛) inspect Google Ads accounts, Google Analytics 4 properties, Merchant Center accounts and Search Console properties that the signed-in user is authorized to access. It is used for the owner's administrative work, including accounts whose owners have delegated access.

Current operating mode: read-only reporting.
Account and report reads are performed locally after Google authorization. Any action that changes an account, product, advertisement or connection configuration requires separate, explicit approval.

Google Ads

Discover accessible advertising accounts and manager accounts; read account and reporting information after the required authentication succeeds.

Google Analytics 4

List authorized properties and web streams, and read aggregated website reporting data.

Merchant Center

Read authorized merchant account, product and product-status information through Google's Merchant API.

Search Console

Read authorized website properties and aggregate search performance for reporting and diagnostics.

How authorization works

  1. The owner starts the desktop utility on their computer.
  2. Google's official authorization page opens in the owner's browser. The user chooses the account and reviews the requested access.
  3. Google returns authorization to a temporary loopback callback on that same computer. The utility stores credentials with Windows user-level encryption.
  4. The utility sends requests directly to Google APIs and saves reports locally for the owner's review.

This documentation website does not run the utility, collect Google credentials or provide a public sign-in service.

Data handling and control

OAuth credentials stay in the owner's encrypted local credential store. Selected report outputs may be processed through OpenAI's Codex when the owner requests assistance. The privacy notice explains this processing, local retention, hosting and how to withdraw access.
